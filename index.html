Understanding the data

Three sources, one spending story

The ABS tracks monthly spending up to 2026. Its survey shows who spends what. Homes Victoria reports local rents.

Important: Survey figures are from 2015–16. Monthly figures run to August 2026. Read them as separate snapshots.

Takeaways

The cost of living is not one number

Housing now dominates the budget. Cities spend more, regions drive further. Age and stress reshape priorities. Your costs depend on who and where you are.
